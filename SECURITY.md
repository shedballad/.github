# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@shedballad.com** with enough detail to reproduce the issue: the affected
endpoint or component, the steps taken, and what you observed. Proof-of-concept
code, requests, or screenshots are welcome.

We acknowledge reports within five business days and will tell you what we
intend to do about it. We ask that you give us a reasonable window to ship a fix
before publishing.

We do not run a paid bounty programme. We do credit reporters who want the
credit.

## Scope

In scope: `shedballad.com` and its public subdomains, and the APIs behind them.

Out of scope:

- Denial of service, volumetric testing, or anything that degrades the service
  for viewers.
- Social engineering of staff, contributors, or partners; physical access.
- Findings from automated scanners with no demonstrated impact - a missing
  header or a version banner is not a report unless you can show the exploit.
- Vulnerabilities in third-party services we consume rather than operate.
- Anything requiring access to another person's account, data, or device. Test
  against accounts you control.

## Versions

Shedballad is an operated service, not distributed software. There are no
supported release branches - the running deployment is the only version, and
fixes ship to it directly.

## Safe harbour

Research conducted in good faith and within this policy is authorised, and we
will not pursue legal action over it. Stay within scope, do not access or
modify data that is not yours, and stop as soon as you have demonstrated the
issue.

If you are unsure whether something is in scope, ask first at the address above.
